Each sleeps on its own runtime's timer: slopio's ticks every 10 ms, tokio's and h2o's every millisecond. h2load keeps as many requests in flight as it has streams, so a server that sleeps less answers more per second for it: lat - slept, the mean latency less the mean sleep, is what the server itself took.
