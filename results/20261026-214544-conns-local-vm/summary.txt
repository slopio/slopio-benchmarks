Medians of 1 runs. asked: connections times the rate each, what h2load aimed for. open: connections established, read half way through. cores: the server's CPU time per second. RSS: the server's resident memory under the load; per conn: what it grew by from idle, over the connections open. kernel TCP: what the machine's TCP buffers grew by, both ends if the client runs there too. client: the load generator's CPUs. Latency: h2load sends every connection's request at the same instant, so it is the time to answer a burst of as many requests as there are connections.
